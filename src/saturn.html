Saturn

Facts about Saturn

  • Saturn has a diameter of about 116,460 km.
  • It is about 1.4 billion km from the Sun.
  • A day lasts about 10.7 hours, and a year about 29.5 Earth years.
  • Its rings are made mostly of chunks of ice and rock.
  • Saturn is less dense than water, and it has the most known moons of any planet.

Tilt the Rings

From Earth, Saturn's rings look wide open in some years and nearly vanish in others.
