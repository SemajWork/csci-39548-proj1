Venus

Facts about Venus

  • Venus is the hottest planet, with surface temperatures around 465 °C.
  • It is about 108 million km from the Sun.
  • Venus spins backward, so the Sun rises in the west there.
  • One rotation takes 243 Earth days, longer than its 225-day year.
  • Venus has no moons, and its thick carbon dioxide air presses about 90 times harder than Earth's.

Earth's Evil Twin

Venus is almost the same size as Earth, which is why it is sometimes called Earth's twin. But underneath its bright clouds, it is one of the most dangerous places in the solar system.

The thick carbon dioxide atmosphere traps heat in a runaway greenhouse effect, making the surface hot enough to melt lead.

The clouds are made of sulfuric acid, and the air pressure on the ground is like being about 900 meters deep in Earth's ocean.

Spacecraft that have landed on Venus survived for only about 2 hours or less before the heat and pressure destroyed them.
