The Sun

Facts about the Sun

  • The Sun is a star, about 4.6 billion years old.
  • Its diameter is about 1.39 million km, roughly 109 times wider than Earth.
  • It holds about 99.8% of all the mass in the solar system.
  • Its surface is about 5,500 °C, and its core reaches about 15 million °C.
  • Sunlight takes about 8 minutes and 20 seconds to reach Earth.

Sun Fun Facts

Want to know some surprising things about our star?

Did you know?

  • About 1.3 million Earths could fit inside the Sun.
  • The Sun is about halfway through its life, with roughly 5 billion years left.
  • Every second, the Sun turns about 4 million tons of matter into energy.
  • The Sun is not on fire. Its light comes from nuclear fusion in its core.
  • The Sun spins faster at its equator (about 25 days) than at its poles (about 35 days).
