Jupiter

Facts about Jupiter

  • Jupiter is the largest planet, with a diameter of about 139,820 km.
  • It is about 778 million km from the Sun.
  • A day on Jupiter lasts only about 10 hours.
  • A year on Jupiter lasts about 12 Earth years.
  • Its Great Red Spot is a storm larger than Earth, and it has more than 90 known moons.

What Would You Weigh on Jupiter?

Jupiter's gravity is about 2.5 times stronger than Earth's.
