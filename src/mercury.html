Mercury

Facts about Mercury

  • Mercury is the smallest planet, with a diameter of about 4,879 km.
  • It is the closest planet to the Sun, about 58 million km away.
  • A year on Mercury lasts only 88 Earth days.
  • Temperatures swing from about 430 °C in the day to about -180 °C at night.
  • Mercury has no moons.
