Exploring Our Solar System

Welcome

Our solar system formed about 4.6 billion years ago from a giant cloud of gas and dust. At its center is the Sun, a star that holds almost all of the system's mass. Eight planets orbit the Sun: the rocky inner planets Mercury, Venus, Earth, and Mars, and the giant outer planets Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune.
