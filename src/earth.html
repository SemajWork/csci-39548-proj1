Earth

Facts about Earth

  • Earth has a diameter of about 12,742 km.
  • It is about 150 million km (1 AU) from the Sun.
  • A day lasts about 24 hours and a year about 365.25 days.
  • About 71% of Earth's surface is covered by water.
  • Earth has one moon and is the only planet known to support life.
