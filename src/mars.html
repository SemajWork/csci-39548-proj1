Mars

Facts about Mars

  • Mars has a diameter of about 6,779 km, about half of Earth's.
  • It is about 228 million km from the Sun.
  • A day on Mars lasts 24 hours and 37 minutes, and a year lasts 687 Earth days.
  • Mars has two small moons: Phobos and Deimos.
  • Olympus Mons on Mars is the tallest volcano in the solar system, about 22 km high.

The Moons of Mars

Phobos (inner) zooms around Mars in under 8 hours. Deimos (outer) takes about 30 hours.
