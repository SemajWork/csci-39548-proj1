The Moon

Facts about the Moon

  • The Moon has a diameter of about 3,474 km.
  • It orbits Earth at an average distance of about 384,400 km.
  • Its gravity is about 1/6 of Earth's.
  • It takes about 27.3 days to orbit Earth, and the same side always faces us.
  • Twelve astronauts walked on the Moon during the Apollo missions (1969–1972).
