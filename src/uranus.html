Uranus

Facts about Uranus

  • Uranus has a diameter of about 50,724 km.
  • It is about 2.9 billion km from the Sun.
  • A day lasts about 17 hours, and a year about 84 Earth years.
  • Uranus is tilted about 98 degrees, so it spins on its side.
  • It is an ice giant with the coldest atmosphere of any planet, down to about -224 °C.

How Old Would You Be on Uranus?

A year on Uranus lasts about 84 Earth years.
